## Decisions & reasoning
* **Mascot Direction Pivot:** Stakeholder's preference for the cool brain mascot + heart wordmark shifts the brand from a formal luxury catalog to an approachable, modern tech platform (Notion/Duolingo/Eventbrite meets weddings).
* **2-Tone & Clean Outlines:** Flat solid fills and clean outlines ensure immediate recognizability at small sizes (16px favicon) and seamless reproduction across merchandise, app headers, and print.
* **VectorCraft Document Integration:** Providing native `.vectorcraft` and `.ai` files allows the user and their design team to continue hand-tuning anchor points directly at home.

## What I tried that didn't work
* **Direct Bitmap Auto-Tracing:** Potrace directly on a 3D brushed-metal photo picked up noise and produced wobbly, soft edges. Fixed by isolating clean color segmentations and reconstructing clean geometric curves.
* **Raw Text Elements in VectorCraft CLI:** System font fallbacks occur when local fonts aren't installed in the CLI environment. Fixed by rendering typography as raw vector paths (`<path d="...">`).
