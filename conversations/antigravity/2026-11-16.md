## Decisions & reasoning
* **Mascot Character Alternatives:** While the brain symbolized "smart planning", an anatomical organ can feel clinical or strange in a romantic wedding context. The Lovebird, Otter, and Knotty options maintain 100% of the cool sunglasses and rings/checklist props while providing a vastly more natural emotional connection for couples and event planners.
* **Cohesive Palette Across All 5 Mascots:** All 5 mascot alternatives share the exact approved brand tokens: Royal Aubergine (`#2E1741`), Bubblegum Pink (`#DB619B`), Sunshine Gold (`#E8B536`), and Warm Linen (`#FAF7EF`), allowing the stakeholder to evaluate the character design purely on its narrative and brand fit.
