Main flow

  1. Home → tap Katering
  2. Adjust the radius (try 10 km for the empty state)
  3. Tick 2–3 vendors → Bandingkan
  4. Open a vendor → tap the Disahkan badge
  5. Hantar permintaan → My Majlis
  6. My Majlis → tap a booked vendor → chat or cancel
  7. Inspirasi tab → swipe up, double-tap to like, tap 💬 to comment
  8. Switch to Guest → tap Book or ♡ to see login + OTP
