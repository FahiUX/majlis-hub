Colour washes (direction, not final tokens)

Green = safe hands

One deep green for actions and trust marks. It feels steady and halal-friendly, and it isn't flashy.

Gold = celebration

Borrowed from songket and pelamin. Only for joyful moments like the countdown and "Top rated". Never for buttons.

Photos do the shouting

Vendors' real work is the hero. The interface stays white and paper so the pelamin, food and couples can shine.
